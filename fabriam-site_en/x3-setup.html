Fabrikam Fly X3 Setup Guide

This guide walks you through unboxing, initial configuration, and pre-first-flight checks. Following these steps will help you start your first on-site operation safely and smoothly. All flights must comply with aviation law and other applicable regulations.

Home / Setup Guide

Last updated: July 1, 2026

This guide summarizes the key points of the official manual (compatible with FBK-FLY-X3 User Manual v1.2). For detailed diagrams and safety notices, refer to the included booklet and the document list on the Support & Maintenance Services page.

STEP 1. Checking the Contents (Unboxing)

After unboxing, first confirm that the following items are all included. If there is any damage or missing item from shipping, do not fly the unit and contact Support.

  • Fabrikam Fly X3 aircraft unit (model FBK-FLY-X3)
  • Propellers (including spares) / dedicated transmitter (controller)
  • Intelligent battery, charger, and power cable
  • microSD card, USB cable, and Quick Start Guide (Japanese)
  • (Optional accessories) Reinforced dust/water-resistant case / spare high-capacity battery / thermal calibration kit
Inspect the propellers every time for deformation, cracks, or chips. Always replace damaged propellers before flight.

STEP 2. Charging

  1. Fully charge the intelligent battery using the included charger. Charging may take longer in low-temperature environments.
  2. Likewise, fully charge the transmitter (controller).
  3. For the first use, charge both the aircraft and transmitter to at least 80% to ensure sufficient power for the firmware update.
The operating temperature range is -20°C to 50°C (catalog value measured under Fabrikam's test conditions; actual results vary by conditions). We recommend charging at room temperature.

STEP 3. Installing the "Fabrikam Flight" App and Linking Your Account

  1. Install the "Fabrikam Flight" operation app on your mobile device.
  2. Sign in with your Fabrikam account (or create a new one if you don't have one).
  3. If you are using an organization account, link to Fabrikam InsightCloud with the permissions (role) granted by your administrator. See Security for details on authentication and permission management.

STEP 4. Aircraft Activation and Firmware Update

  1. Power on the transmitter and aircraft, then activate the aircraft by following the app's instructions.
  2. Check the firmware version. The current version is v1.2; v1.3 is scheduled for release in July 2026.
  3. If prompted to update (v1.2 → v1.3), wait for it to complete without powering off. Updates are delivered with verified, signed authenticity (see Security for details).
Do not disconnect power or communication while a firmware update is in progress. See Troubleshooting for steps to take if an update fails.

STEP 5. Configuring RTK Positioning

The X3 supports high-precision positioning via GNSS + RTK (horizontal ±1cm + 1ppm / practical ±2cm, catalog values). Configure one of the following depending on your use case.

  • Network RTK: Register the connection information for the correction data service in the app.
  • RTK base station: Set up your own base station and connect it to the aircraft.

STEP 6. Calibration

  1. Compass (magnetic) calibration: Perform this in an open area away from metal and magnetic sources, following the app's instructions.
  2. IMU calibration: Perform this with the aircraft placed on a level surface.
  3. Thermal calibration (optional): Perform this using the thermal calibration kit. Thermal readings are approximate temperature indicators and should not be used to make definitive quantitative diagnoses.

STEP 7. Pre-First-Flight Checklist

Confirm all of the following before your first flight.

Check itemDetails
Flight airspace & permitsConfirm the airspace is permitted for flight and that any required permits/approvals are in place (lawful operation is a prerequisite)
Aircraft registration & Remote IDAircraft registration is complete, the registration number is displayed, and Remote ID is enabled
GNSS acquisitionSufficient satellites acquired outdoors and RTK is Fixed
BatterySufficient charge remaining for both aircraft and transmitter (with margin for a round trip)
Propellers & aircraftPropellers properly attached and undamaged, gimbal lock removed, no visible abnormalities
Surrounding environmentDistance from people, structures, and obstacles; wind speed (12 m/s wind resistance: catalog value); and weather conditions confirmed
Aircraft registration, display of the registration number, and installation/activation of Remote ID are requirements under aviation law. See Compliance & Certifications for details, and ensure lawful operation at all times.
