Fabrikam Fly X3 Support FAQ

Here are answers to frequently asked questions about the Fabrikam Fly X3, organized by category. Each answer links to the related detail page.

Last updated: July 1, 2026

Before You Buy: Specifications

Q. Is the thermal camera included as standard?

A. Yes. A radiometric 640×512 thermal camera is included as standard. It lets you visualize temperature anomalies (water leaks, insulation defects, electrical faults, etc.) at no additional cost. See Features for detailed specifications.

Q. Can thermal imaging provide a quantitative temperature diagnosis?

A. Thermal images are intended as a general guide to temperature. Displayed temperatures depend on environmental conditions such as calibration status, emissivity, distance, and weather, so they should not be treated as a definitive quantitative diagnosis.

Q. How accurate is the positioning?

A. GNSS + RTK is supported, with a practical horizontal accuracy of ±2 cm (catalog value measured under our test conditions; actual results vary). This is useful for year-over-year comparison of the same location.

Operation

Q. Can AI crack detection results be used directly in an inspection report?

A. AI crack detection is an assistive feature that reduces manual effort in extracting cracks. Validating the extracted results and making the final inspection determination requires confirmation by a qualified professional.

Q. Can it fly in rainy weather?

A. It is rated IP55 for dust/water resistance and can withstand winds up to 12 m/s (both catalog values). However, this does not guarantee unlimited weather resistance; flights must comply with applicable regulations and safety checks, and flying should be avoided in severe weather. See Operating Procedures for the basic workflow.

Q. How long can it fly on a single flight?

A. Maximum flight time is 55 minutes (catalog value under no-wind, standard-payload conditions). Actual results vary depending on wind, payload, temperature, and flight mode. Always operate with a margin of safety.

Regulations and Certification

Q. Does it comply with technical conformity certification under the Radio Act (Giteki)?

A. Yes, it does. For details, as well as Civil Aeronautics Act compliance (aircraft registration, remote ID), see Compliance & Certification. All flights must comply with applicable laws.

Q. Is aircraft registration or remote ID required?

A. Under the Civil Aeronautics Act, aircraft registration, display of the registration number, and remote ID are required. Before operating, review Compliance & Certification and Setup Instructions.

Maintenance and Support

Q. How long is the warranty period?

A. The aircraft warranty is 12 months. See Support & Maintenance Services for our maintenance policy (inspection, repair, RMA, loaner units) and replacement parts supply.

Q. What happens to support for older models?

A. The original X1 is scheduled to reach end of support at the end of March 2027. We recommend the X3 as the successor to the X2. Our lifecycle/EOL policy is described on Support & Maintenance Services.

Cloud and Integration

Q. What is the cloud service's uptime?

A. Fabrikam InsightCloud targets a monthly uptime of 99.5% or higher. See Service Level Agreement (SLA) for details.

Q. Can it integrate with our existing inspection management system?

A. Yes, integration is possible via SDK/API. See System Integration: SDK/API for supported data formats and extensibility.

Q. How is captured data handled, and what about privacy?

A. See our Privacy Statement for our policy on handling personal information and video data, and Security for safeguards such as encryption and access control.

If this doesn't resolve your issue, check Troubleshooting and then contact our support desk.
