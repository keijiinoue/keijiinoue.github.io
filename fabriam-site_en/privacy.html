Privacy Statement

This page explains how Fabrikam handles personal information, video, and inspection data across our products and services.

Home / Privacy Statement

Last updated: July 1, 2026

1. Scope

This statement applies to the handling of personal information and captured/inspection data by the industrial inspection drone, the flight control application "Fabrikam Flight," and the cloud service "Fabrikam InsightCloud" (collectively, the "Service") provided by Fabrikam Co., Ltd.

2. Information We Collect (NFR-10)

  • Account Information: Registration details such as name, company name, email address, phone number, and login ID.
  • Captured/Inspection Data: Visible-light video and still images, thermal (temperature) data, location data (geotags), and generated inspection reports.
  • Usage Logs: System logs such as operation history, flight logs, access timestamps, and device/network environment.

Captured data may include people, vehicles, or other subjects. Ensuring the lawfulness of image capture and obtaining any necessary consent is the responsibility of the customer operating the Service.

3. Purpose of Use

Information collected is used to provide and maintain the Service, generate inspection reports and AI analysis, enable features such as change-over-time comparison, support quality improvement and issue resolution, provide customer support, and communicate matters related to the contract. We will not use this information beyond these purposes except as required by law.

4. Third-Party Disclosure & Management of Subcontractors (NFR-10)

We do not, in principle, provide collected personal information or captured data to third parties, except where required by law or where the individual has given consent. Where subcontracting is necessary for the operation of the Service, we carefully select subcontractors, impose appropriate security and confidentiality obligations by contract, and supervise their handling of the data.

5. Data Storage Location & Cross-Border Transfer (NFR-11)

Data stored in Fabrikam InsightCloud can be stored in a domestic region of your choice. By default, data is stored in the storage region selected at the time of contract, and its location (domestic or international) is clearly indicated. If a cross-border transfer is involved, we will inform the customer in advance and implement appropriate protective measures in accordance with applicable laws.

6. Retention Period, Deletion, and Return of Data (NFR-12)

Data is retained during the contract term and for any retention period specified in the contract. Upon customer request, we support export (return) and deletion of stored data. At the end of the contract, data will be returned or erased according to predefined procedures. For deletion or return requests, please contact the support desk below.

7. Security Measures

We implement technical and organizational security measures including encryption of data in transit and at rest, access control (role-based permission management and support for multi-factor authentication), and audit logging. For details, see our Security page.

8. User Rights

Users may request disclosure, correction, suspension of use, or deletion of their personal information held by us. We may verify your identity when processing such requests.

9. Contact

For inquiries regarding this statement or requests for disclosure, please contact our support desk (support@fabrikam.example.com / 0120-xxx-xxx).
