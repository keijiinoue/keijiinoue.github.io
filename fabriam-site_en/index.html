About Fabrikam

Turning what's invisible on-site into reliable data.

Fabrikam Co., Ltd. is a manufacturer that develops and provides industrial inspection drones together with "Fabrikam InsightCloud," a cloud platform that handles inspection data end to end. At inspection sites for social infrastructure such as bridges, power lines, transmission towers, and plant facilities, safety risks from working at height and in close proximity, heavy labor and time requirements, and inconsistent record quality have long been challenges. By providing the aircraft, sensors, AI analysis, and cloud as an integrated whole, we help improve safety, reduce staffing needs, standardize records, and make better use of data.

Three Reasons Customers Choose the Fabrikam Fly X3

Standard Thermal Camera

Equipped standard with a 640×512 radiometric thermal camera. Visualizes temperature anomalies — such as water leaks, insulation defects, and electrical faults — as a temperature reference. Expands inspection coverage at no additional cost.

AI Crack Detection for Labor Savings

Automatically extracts cracks in concrete structures, reducing the effort of report preparation. The extracted results are supplementary information; final judgments are assumed to be made through inspection by a qualified professional.

RTK Positioning — Practical Accuracy ±2 cm

GNSS plus RTK pinpoints inspection locations with precision, supporting year-over-year comparisons and reproducible inspections (catalog values; subject to variation based on conditions).
