Fabrikam Fly X3 Troubleshooting

This page lists common symptoms along with their likely causes and remedies. If the issue does not improve after taking these steps, do not continue flying the aircraft; contact Support instead.

Last updated: July 1, 2026

If the issue persists after taking the steps listed here, keep the aircraft in a safe state and contact Support & Maintenance Services (Japanese-language support, weekdays 9:00-18:00).

Flight and Positioning Issues

SymptomPossible causeRemedy
GNSS/RTK will not FixInsufficient satellite acquisition, overhead obstruction, RTK correction data not received, or incorrect base station/network settingsMove to an open outdoor area and wait a few minutes. Recheck the RTK connection information and base station settings (Setup Guide STEP 5)
Unstable transmission / corrupted videoNon-line-of-sight conditions, radio interference, distance exceeded, or improper antenna orientationEnsure line of sight and point the antenna toward the aircraft. Maximum transmission range is 15 km (catalog value, within line-of-sight and legal limits). Reduce distance and avoid interference sources
Cannot take offCalibration incomplete, gimbal lock still attached, compass error, or within a geofenceRecalibrate the compass/IMU, remove the gimbal lock, and confirm the airspace is permitted for flight
Aircraft drifts / attitude unstableStrong wind (exceeding 12 m/s wind resistance), magnetic interference, or IMU errorCheck wind speed and cancel the flight in adverse conditions. Move away from magnetic sources and recalibrate

Power and Battery Issues

SymptomPossible causeRemedy
Battery error warningLow/high temperature, degradation, dirty contacts, or insufficient chargeReturn to room temperature, clean the contacts, and recheck after a full charge. If this does not help, replace with a spare battery
Will not power onBattery fully depleted or improperly attachedCharge the battery and attach it securely. Use within the operating temperature range of -20°C to 50°C (catalog value)

Imaging, AI, and Cloud Issues

SymptomPossible causeRemedy
Thermal image looks wrongCalibration drift, emissivity/distance conditions, dirty lens, or effects of ambient temperaturePerform thermal calibration and clean the lens. Readings are an approximate temperature guide only and should not be used for definitive quantitative diagnosis
AI crack detection does not workSubject out of scope, insufficient image quality, or conditions unsupported by the featureRecapture at sufficient resolution and proximity. AI is a supporting feature to reduce manual effort; final judgment requires confirmation by a qualified inspector
Cannot upload to InsightCloudNetwork unavailable, expired authentication, insufficient permissions, or insufficient storage spaceCheck your connection, sign-in status, and permissions (Security). Retry, and if the issue persists check your integration settings
Firmware update failsCommunication lost, low power, or operation performed during the updateRetry with sufficient charge and a stable connection. Do not disconnect power or communication during the update. Contact Support if the issue is not resolved

If the aircraft or battery shows any safety-related abnormality such as smoke, deformation, unusual noise, or signs of a fall, stop using it immediately and do not fly it. Contact Support & Maintenance Services (RMA / loaner unit available).
