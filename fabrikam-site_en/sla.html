Service Level Agreement (SLA)

This page describes the quality targets for cloud services including Fabrikam InsightCloud, and our response policy in the event of an outage.

Home / Service Level Agreement (SLA)

Last updated: July 1, 2026

Scope of Application

This SLA applies to cloud services provided by Fabrikam, such as "Fabrikam InsightCloud." It does not cover offline functions of the aircraft or app, or events caused by telecommunications carriers or the customer's own network or equipment.

Uptime Target (NFR-07)

The monthly uptime target is 99.5% or higher. Uptime is calculated based on the total monthly time for the covered service, minus scheduled maintenance time and downtime due to the exclusions listed in the table below.

CategoryDetails
Calculation basisThe percentage of service availability relative to (total monthly time − scheduled maintenance time − downtime due to exclusions)
Scheduled maintenanceMaintenance time that has been announced in advance
ExclusionsForce majeure, issues with the customer's network or equipment, services outside our control, and events caused by use outside the intended usage

Advance Notice of Scheduled Maintenance (NFR-08)

When we perform scheduled maintenance, we will, as a general rule, provide advance notice of the date/time and scope of impact at least 5 business days in advance. This may not apply in unavoidable circumstances, such as urgent security response.

Support Response & Recovery Targets in the Event of an Outage (NFR-09)

When we receive a report of an outage, we respond according to the following target response times based on severity (within support business hours; recovery targets may vary depending on the situation).

SeverityDefinitionTarget Response Time
Critical (S1)The entire service is down, with a significant impact on business continuityWithin 1 business hour
High (S2)A major function is unavailable (no workaround)Within 4 business hours
Medium (S3)A function has a defect, but a workaround existsWithin 1 business day
Low (S4)Minor defects or questionsWithin 2 business days

Support Desk Business Hours

Support is provided in Japanese, weekdays 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM (excluding weekends, public holidays, and year-end/New Year holidays). Inquiries received outside business hours will be handled during the next business hours.

Service Credits (Reference)

For reference, the approach to service credits when monthly uptime falls short of the target is as follows. Applicable conditions and limits are defined in the individual contract.

Monthly UptimeService Credit (percentage of monthly fee, for reference)

Below 99.0% and at or above 95.0%10%
