Product Overview

Model FBK-FLY-X3 | Industrial Inspection Drone (Quadcopter)

The "Fabrikam Fly X3" is our new flagship, condensing the imaging, positioning, autonomous flight, and data integration needed for infrastructure inspection into a single unit. In addition to a visible-light camera, it comes standard with a thermal camera, high-precision RTK positioning, up to 55 minutes of flight time, and six-directional obstacle avoidance. From imaging to inspection report creation, AI analysis, and year-over-year comparison, everything can be handled end to end through "Fabrikam InsightCloud." Shipping began June 10, 2026.

Standard Thermal Camera RTK — Practical ±2 cm Up to 55 min Flight IP55 6-Direction Obstacle Avoidance

Challenges It Addresses

  • Inspecting hard-to-reach locations such as under bridge girders, elevated sites, and transmission towers
  • The labor and time burden of inspections, along with safety risks from scaffolding and working at height
  • Inconsistent inspection records that make year-over-year comparison and reproducibility difficult
  • Underutilization of imaging data within existing inspection management systems

Three Values the X3 Delivers

Standard Thermal Camera

Equipped standard with a 640×512 radiometric thermal camera. Visualizes temperature anomalies — such as water leaks, insulation defects, and electrical faults — as a temperature reference. Quantitative diagnosis depends on calibration and environmental conditions, so please use it as a reference only.

AI Crack Detection for Labor Savings

Automatically extracts cracks in concrete structures, reducing the effort of report preparation. The extracted results are supplementary information supporting inspection; final judgments are assumed to be made through inspection by a qualified professional.

RTK Positioning — Practical Accuracy ±2 cm

GNSS plus RTK pinpoints inspection locations with precision, supporting year-over-year comparisons of the same location and reproducible inspections.

Key Use Cases

Bridge Inspection

Assists close visual inspection of girder undersides, elevated areas, and bearings, reducing the need for working at height.

Power Line & Tower Inspection

Efficiently photographs transmission towers and overhead lines. Thermal imaging visualizes signs of electrical faults.

Plant Facility Inspection

Simultaneously records temperature distribution and appearance when inspecting piping, tanks, chimneys, and more.

Surveying & As-Built Verification

Positional records leveraging RTK positioning support part of surveying and as-built management work.

Disaster Prevention & Response

Can be used for remote assessment of hazardous areas, such as understanding conditions at disaster sites.
