Compliance, Certification & Technical Conformity

Fabrikam Fly X3 is a product intended to be operated in compliance with applicable laws, including aviation regulations. This page covers aircraft registration and remote ID, radio law technical conformity, and our quality and environmental management systems.

Home / Compliance & Certification

Last updated: July 1, 2026

This page provides an overview of the mechanisms supported by the product. For actual flights, operators must independently confirm whether permits or approvals are required based on the airspace, time of day, and manner of flight, and must ensure lawful operation at all times.

Compliance with Aviation Law (Aircraft Registration & Remote ID)

Fabrikam Fly X3 supports operation in compliance with aviation law. (NFR-17)

  • Aircraft Registration: Supports the registration process and allows display of the registration number on the aircraft.
  • Remote ID: Equipped with remote ID functionality that can be enabled for operation.
  • Registration and remote ID setup steps are included in the pre-flight checklist in Setup Instructions.

Approach to Flight Rule Compliance

Depending on the manner of flight—such as flying in no-fly zones or areas requiring permission, nighttime flight, beyond visual line of sight flight, or flight over third parties—permits or approvals may be required. Operators must confirm their flight plan in advance and complete any necessary procedures before flying. The aircraft's geofencing settings help prevent unintended entry into restricted airspace.

Radio Law: Technical Conformity Certification

The wireless equipment in this product has obtained Technical Conformity Certification under the Radio Law. Wireless functionality, including the Fabrikam Link transmission method, is available in a state that conforms to technical standards. (NFR-18)

The display of the conformity mark and the scope of applicability conform to the product labeling and accompanying documentation (all values stated in this demo are fictitious).

Quality & Environmental Management

Fabrikam maintains a management system for product quality and environmental responsibility. We pursue continuous improvement across design, manufacturing, and maintenance processes based on frameworks equivalent to international standards (quality: equivalent to ISO 9001; environment: equivalent to ISO 14001). (NFR-19)

Standards Conformity (EMC & Safety)

The product is designed with consideration for electromagnetic compatibility (EMC) and electrical safety standards conformity. It has an IP55 (catalog value) dust and water resistance rating, ensuring operational reliability outdoors and under adverse conditions. For details on environmental specifications, see Features & Specifications.

General Considerations Regarding Export and Legal Matters

When handling the product or its technology, please comply with applicable export control laws and other relevant regulations. Use overseas or re-export may be subject to local radio and aviation regulations. For questions, please contact our support desk.
