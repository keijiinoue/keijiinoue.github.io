Imaging & Sensors

The visible-light camera supports 4K/8K capture, and combined with a 1/1.3-inch sensor and 3-axis gimbal, records high-resolution stills and video with reduced camera shake. It comes with a 640×512 radiometric thermal camera as standard, enabling dual visible-light and thermal imaging. Thermal imaging visualizes temperature distribution as a reference; quantitative diagnosis depends on calibration and environmental conditions, so please use it as a reference only.

Positioning & Accuracy

In addition to GNSS, the X3 supports RTK, achieving high-precision positioning of horizontal ±1 cm + 1 ppm (practical accuracy ±2 cm). Captured data can be geotagged with location information, supporting pinpoint recording of inspection sites and year-over-year comparison.

Flight Performance

Maximum flight time is 55 minutes (no wind, standard payload), with a maximum flight speed of 23 m/s (S-mode). Wind resistance is rated at 12 m/s, and the operating temperature range is -20°C to 50°C. The transmission system, "Fabrikam Link," supports a range of up to 15 km (line-of-sight, within legal limits). Dust and water resistance is rated IP55, designed for outdoor use and harsh conditions. All figures are catalog values and will vary depending on conditions.

Autonomy & Safety

The X3 supports waypoint-based automatic flight and automated patrol route configuration, enabling highly reproducible inspection routes. It features 6-direction vision plus ToF obstacle avoidance, automatic return-to-home (failsafe/RTH), and geofencing (control of no-fly zones). Flight operations require compliance with applicable laws and regulations, including aviation law.

AI & Analysis

AI crack detection automatically extracts cracks in concrete structures, reducing the labor required for report preparation. The extracted results are supplementary information supporting inspection, and final judgments are assumed to be made through inspection by a qualified professional. The system also supports automatic generation of draft inspection reports from captured data, as well as time-series comparison (year-over-year comparison) of the same location.

Data & Integration

In addition to 128 GB of built-in storage, the X3 supports microSD cards. Captured data can be stored and managed in "Fabrikam InsightCloud," enabling an end-to-end workflow from imaging to automatic inspection report generation, AI analysis, and year-over-year comparison. An SDK/API allows integration with existing inspection management and core business systems.
