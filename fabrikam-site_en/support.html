Product Warranty

The Fabrikam Fly X3 aircraft warranty period is 12 months. It covers manufacturing defects under normal use in accordance with the instruction manual. Consumables, and defects caused by accidents, modifications, or improper operation, may be excluded from warranty coverage. (NFR-13)

Maintenance Menu (Inspection, Repair, RMA, Loaner Units)

  • Scheduled inspection: Functional checks of the aircraft and sensors, and preventive maintenance.
  • Repair: Diagnosis of the fault and replacement of parts.
  • RMA (return merchandise authorization): Our standard flow of return, diagnosis, and repair/replacement after intake.
  • Loaner units: We provide loaner units, subject to conditions, to support business continuity during the repair period.
RMA procedures and required information will be provided by our support desk. Do not fly any aircraft exhibiting a safety-related anomaly, and contact us promptly. (NFR-14)

Replacement Parts Supply

Replacement parts are supplied for a defined period (approximately 5 years) after the end of production of the relevant product. This can be factored into maintenance planning for long-term operation. (NFR-15)

Training & Education

We provide training for pilots, supporting safe operation and standardized quality in inspection work.

  • Basic course: Basic operation of the aircraft and app, safe operation, and fundamentals of lawful operation.
  • Practical course: Hands-on practice with automated patrol/inspection route setup, imaging, and data utilization.
  • Certification: Certification of operational skills for those who complete the designated curriculum.

All flights must comply with applicable regulations such as the Civil Aeronautics Act. See Compliance & Certification for related requirements. (NFR-22)

Documentation (Japanese)

  • Operating Procedures (basic operation, automated patrol, imaging, and return-to-home)
  • Troubleshooting (symptom → cause → resolution)
  • Features & Specifications / System Integration: SDK/API
