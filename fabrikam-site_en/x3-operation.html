Fabrikam Fly X3 Operation Guide

This guide explains the standard operating workflow, from basic operation through automated patrol/inspection routes, visible-light/thermal imaging, and automatic return. All flights must comply with aviation law and other applicable regulations.

Home / Operation Guide

Last updated: July 1, 2026

Mandatory pre-flight checks (lawful operation): Always confirm that the flight airspace is permitted for flight, that any required permits/approvals are in place, that the aircraft is registered with its registration number displayed, and that Remote ID is enabled. See Compliance & Certifications for details. Responsibility for safe operation rests with the pilot.

1. Pre-Flight Preparation

  1. Complete the pre-first-flight checklist in the Setup Guide.
  2. Check the aircraft's status (battery, GNSS/RTK, available storage) in the "Fabrikam Flight" app.
  3. Choose a flat, open area for takeoff and landing, and maintain a safe distance from surrounding people and structures.

2. Basic Operation

Takeoff and Hovering

  1. Power on the transmitter, then the aircraft, and confirm that GNSS/RTK is Fixed.
  2. Take off automatically or with manual throttle, and hover at low altitude for a few seconds to confirm the aircraft is stable.

Flight and Capture

  1. Move to the target inspection site. In S-mode, speeds of up to 23 m/s (catalog value; varies by conditions) are possible.
  2. Adjust the camera angle with the gimbal (3-axis image stabilization) to capture photos and video.

Automatic Return to Home (RTH)

You can start automatic return to home (RTH) with the Home button. The aircraft will return automatically as a failsafe if the battery runs low or communication is lost. Watch for obstacles along the return path and intervene manually if necessary.

Maximum flight time is 55 minutes (catalog value under no-wind, standard-payload conditions). Actual flight time will vary with wind, payload, temperature, and flight style. Always operate with a safety margin.

3. Automated Patrol and Inspection Routes

Automated patrol is effective for recurring inspection targets such as bridges, power lines, and plant facilities.

  1. Set waypoints in the app and specify altitude, speed, and camera actions.
  2. Save the inspection route as a template so you can reproduce the same flight path for consistent captures on subsequent runs.
  3. RTK ±2cm (catalog value) allows highly repeatable recording of the same location, useful for tracking changes over time.
  4. Use obstacle detection/avoidance (6-direction vision + ToF) together with geofencing to maintain a safety margin.
Even during automated patrol, the pilot must continuously monitor the aircraft and remain ready to intervene manually at any time. Autonomous features do not replace the pilot's judgment or oversight.

4. Visible-Light and Thermal Imaging and Switching

You can switch between visible light (4K/8K, 1/1.3-inch sensor) and thermal (640×512, with radiometric measurement, standard equipment), or capture both simultaneously in dual mode.

  • Select the appropriate camera for the inspection target, and record visible light and thermal simultaneously as needed.
  • Use thermal images as a temperature guide only. Displayed temperatures depend on calibration status and environmental conditions (emissivity, distance, weather, etc.) and should not be treated as a definitive quantitative diagnosis.

5. Data Recording and Uploading to InsightCloud

  1. Captured data is recorded to 128GB of built-in storage plus a microSD card, with location (geotag) information attached.
  2. After the flight, uploading to Fabrikam InsightCloud enables end-to-end automatic inspection report generation, AI analysis, and comparison over time.
  3. AI crack detection is a supporting feature that reduces manual effort in extracting cracks. Validity of the extraction results and the final inspection determination require confirmation by a qualified inspector.

6. Safety Notes

  • Maintain sufficient distance from people, structures, vehicles, power lines, and similar obstacles, and avoid flying over third parties.
  • Refrain from flying in bad weather such as strong wind, rain, lightning, or poor visibility (12 m/s wind resistance and IP55 are catalog values and do not guarantee unlimited weather resistance).
  • Always maintain a margin of battery charge, and return and land promptly when a warning appears.
  • In an emergency, prioritize an immediate landing in a safe location rather than forcing the flight to continue.
If a problem occurs, see Troubleshooting; for common questions, see the Support FAQ.
